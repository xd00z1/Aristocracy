Four independent transcriptions agree on something quite different: Colin Hume's ABC made from the Ricordi vocal score p. 71 (in G: 'GGG | B2F | E3 | D3 | GAB | G2g | d3 | d2B'), Gary Dahl's Mel Bay arrangement and the Wikifonia lead sheet (both in Ab, L:1/8: 'AAA c2G | F3 E3 | ABc A2a | e3 e2c'), and Tim Roberts's Lilypond arrangement ('c8 c c e4 b8 | a4. g4. | c8 d e c4 c'8 | g4.~g4 e8'). In Ab major the opening is therefore Ab4 Ab4 Ab4 C5 G4 | F4 Eb4 ('O mio bab-bi-no | ca-ro'): three repeated eighths on the downbeat, a rise to C5, and a stepwise fall to Eb4, then 'mi pia-ce, e bel-lo' = Ab4 Bb4 C5 Ab4 Ab5 with the octave leap to the high Ab. The file's version starts a fourth too high, has no repeated notes, and never touches the phrase's actual lowest note (Eb4). Drop the Needle would play a tune that is not the aria. Suggested: Replace with the first four bars at concert pitch (Ab major, 6/8): 'Ab4/8 Ab4/8 Ab4/8 C5/4 G4/8 | F4/4. Eb4/4. | Ab4/8 Bb4/8 C5/8 Ab4/4 Ab5/8 | Eb5/4. Eb5/4 C5/8' (14 notes; the last bar's Eb5 Eb5 is 'bel-lo', re-articulated, not tied). Confirm against the Ricordi vocal score before signing off, and update theme.note to say 'first four bars' rather than 'opening phrase only'.
